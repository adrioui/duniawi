# Unslop

Status: stub — full port lands in M2.

Approximate behavior until then:

- Scan for tells, rewrite preserving meaning, add soul (opinions, varied rhythm, first person, specificity), then self-audit: "what makes this obviously AI generated?"
- Kill content tells: puffery, name-dropping, superficial -ing phrases, promotional language, vague attributions, formulaic challenges.
- Kill language tells: AI vocabulary (delve, crucial, landscape), fancy ways to say "is", "not just X but Y", forced rule of three, synonym cycling, false ranges.
- Kill style tells: em dashes entirely (periods or commas only), colon as mid-sentence connector, bold overuse, inline-header lists, title case, decorative emojis, curly quotes.
- Kill chat and filler tells: "I hope this helps!", cutoff disclaimers, sycophancy, filler phrases, excessive hedging, generic conclusions, abstract metaphor nouns (substrate, leverage, endgame).
- Say what it does, not how it feels; short sentences; active voice; cut adverbs; prefer the plain word. Applies to every reply, every playbook, and the decision trail too.
